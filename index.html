Immigration

See Our Immigrations

Visa route

From visa preparation to departure.

  1. Visa Eligibility Check

    Review the destination's visa criteria and your personal circumstances.

  2. Financial & Document Preparation

    Prepare proof of funds and other required supporting documents.

  3. Visa Application & Submission

    Complete the relevant application and submit the required documents.

  4. Biometrics & Interview

    Attend appointments or interviews if required.

  5. VFS Appointment

    Schedule your VFS appointment for visa processing.

  6. Visa Decision

    Receive the decision from the relevant immigration authority.

  7. Pre-Departure Preparation

    Prepare your travel documents, accommodation and arrival plans.

Application route

Your journey to securing admission abroad.

  1. Profile Assessment

    Evaluate your academic background, goals and eligibility.

  2. Course & University Selection

    Choose the right destination, course and institution.

  3. Document Preparation

    Organise academic records and required supporting documents.

  4. Application Submission

    Submit your application to the selected institution.

  5. Offer Letter & Admission

    Receive and review your admission offer.

  6. Fee Payment & Next Steps

    Complete applicable admission formalities and prepare for the visa stage.
