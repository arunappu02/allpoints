We offer high-quality teaching and mentoring services to help you achieve your academic and professional goals, with personalized guidance from expert instructors tailored to your needs for success.

Who We Are?

Driven by Originality and Superiority

AllPoints Ltd is dedicated to providing comprehensive educational support and services across multiple countries, including Italy, Malta, France, Germany, Ireland, the UK, and Canada. With a commitment to excellence in education, AllPoints offers a range of resources and assistance tailored to meet the diverse needs of students in these regions.

Whether it’s facilitating enrollment in courses, providing tutoring, offering educational materials, or consulting on academic matters, AllPoints strives to empower learners to achieve their educational goals. Through personalized guidance and a deep understanding of local educational landscapes, AllPoints aims to foster success and enhance learning experiences for students across borders.

Our Mission

Study abroad is not a dream anymore, it is a path everyone can achieve. We are on a mission to educate the same.

Our Vision

Allpoints envisions expanding operations to cover a wider region and collaborating with cross-border agencies to provide ambitious job seekers with exciting opportunities in elite companies and small businesses. We prioritize the safety, rights, and well-being of our workforce, ensuring they are respected and rewarded.

Our Promise

Your future matters to us. From your first steps abroad to preparing for employment after graduation, AllPoints is here to guide you towards the next chapter of your journey.
